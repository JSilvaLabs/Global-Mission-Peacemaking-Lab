1. Cultural Imperialism (Error)

Uncritical rejection of host culture: importing donor-culture architecture, melodies, and social customs as mandatory conditions for salvation (cf. Acts 15:1 error), making the Gospel feel like a foreign religion and driving local customs underground.

2. Critical Contextualization (Biblical)

Paul Hiebert's 4-step ecclesial discernment: (1) study cultural form deeply, (2) exegete Scripture faithfully, (3) evaluate cultural meanings in local congregation, and (4) create redeemed indigenous Christian practices that honor Christ.

3. Syncretism (Error)

Uncritical accommodation: blending Christ with spirit-appeasement, polytheism, ethno-nationalism, or moral relativism (cf. Acts 14:11–13; Col 2:8), hollowing out exclusive allegiance to the risen Lord.
